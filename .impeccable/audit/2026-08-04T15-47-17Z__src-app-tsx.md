**Branch:** `critique-fixes` at `f56dee7a`

**Method:** static source analysis, computed contrast over the real token values

## Implementation Integrity Verdict

**Pass.** The detector reports 4 anti-patterns, all in `src/screens/Onboarding.tsx`
and all four already recorded as documented exceptions in
`.impeccable/critique/ignore.md`: three `#000` literals inside `mask-image` alpha
gradients where the color channel is never painted, and the 27px display heading
that belongs to the separate onboarding window rather than the popover ramp. Zero
surviving findings.

The implementation is not interchangeable with another product. Evidence:

- `gc-switch-off` is `#868c9e` because `#8b91a6` measured 3.13:1 on white but
  2.98:1 on `gc-subtle`, which is the surface an expanded member row uses
  (`tailwind.config.ts:149-153`). Someone tuned the most-used control in the app
  against the second surface it lands on.
- The `gc-*` palette is namespaced specifically so it cannot leak into the shared
  `cg` ink and brand ramps, with the constraint written down as The Provisional
  Indigo Rule.
- `buildGroups` separates `desired` from `routed` because collapsing them once
  made a switch render off and send `!enabled` = false, disabling the thing the
  user was enabling (`groups.test.ts:189-200`).
- 168 tests across 14 files, most of them pinning a specific past failure rather
  than a happy path.

## Detailed Findings by Severity

### [P1] The credential field has no visible focus indicator

- **Location:** `src/components/gc/ui.tsx:165-171`; call sites `src/screens/FirstRun.tsx:169`, `src/screens/Settings.tsx:449`
- **Category:** Accessibility
- **Evidence:** The `<input>` carries `outline-none`. The only focus affordance is
  the wrapper's `focus-within:shadow-border-hover`, which moves the seam hairline
  from 6% ink-800 to 9% ink-800 over white. Composited: `#f0f0f0` to `#e8e8e8`, a
  ratio of **1.06:1** against each other and 1.12:1 vs 1.19:1 against the white
  card. The global `button:focus-visible` rule in `index.css:27-30` does not apply
  to inputs.
- **Impact:** A keyboard user tabbing into either key field cannot tell they have
  arrived. This is the field that takes a live gateway key, and the `secret` prop
  masks the value, so there is no caret-in-text confirmation either.
- **WCAG:** 2.4.7 Focus Visible (AA); the 3:1 indicator threshold in 1.4.11
- **Recommendation:** Give the wrapper the same 2px `gc-accent` ring the buttons
  use, via `focus-within:outline focus-within:outline-2 focus-within:outline-offset-2 focus-within:outline-gc-accent`. That reuses the app's single focus vocabulary rather than inventing a second one, and it is the reason `index.css` centralized the ring in the first place.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Text cannot be resized to 200%

- **Location:** `src-tauri/tauri.conf.json:17-19`; 130 `text-[Npx]` classes across `src/`
- **Category:** Responsive Design / Accessibility
- **Evidence:** `"width": 380, "height": 620, "resizable": false`. Tauri's
  `zoomHotkeysEnabled` is not set, so Ctrl/Cmd+= does nothing. Every font size in
  the app is an absolute px literal: 27 at 11.5px, 25 at 12.5px, 20 at 11px, 19 at
  10.5px, 3 at 10px, and no `rem` anywhere. There is no media query, no user
  setting, and no keyboard path that makes the type larger.
- **Impact:** A low-vision user has one escape hatch, the platform's minimum-font-size
  preference (Chromium and WKWebView both honor it, and it does override px). When
  that fires, the app's 18 `truncate` and `line-clamp` clauses drop content rather
  than wrap it: the gateway host, the org name, tool names, and the credential
  promise in the footer all shorten silently, and `overflow-x-hidden` at
  `App.tsx:1137` guarantees clipping instead of a scrollbar. The vertical growth is
  survivable because the body scrolls.
- **WCAG:** 1.4.4 Resize Text (AA). Note the conformance note that user-agent zoom
  can satisfy this: here there is no user-agent zoom to rely on.
- **Recommendation:** Two independent moves, either of which helps. (a) Enable
  `zoomHotkeysEnabled` and let the window grow with it, which conflicts with the
  fixed-popover geometry and needs a product decision. (b) Convert the type ramp to
  `rem` against a root size the app can raise, and let the 18 truncation sites wrap
  to two lines instead of clipping, the way the ledger exception line already does
  after `line-clamp-2`. (b) is the smaller change and does not touch the window.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

### [P1] The Success confirmation check is 2.10:1

- **Location:** `src/screens/Success.tsx:37-39`
- **Category:** Accessibility
- **Evidence:** `bg-gc-success` (`#2ecc71`) with a white check glyph:
  **2.10:1**. The badge's own edge against the white card is the same 2.10:1, and
  the `ring-2 ring-white` between them adds no contrast.
- **Impact:** The check is the entire visual difference between "you're connected"
  and the same hex mark on First Run. On a bright screen it reads as a green blob;
  the heading carries the meaning and the badge contributes nothing. This is also
  the one screen whose job is reassurance.
- **WCAG:** 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (AA), 3:1 for meaningful graphics
- **Recommendation:** Put the glyph on `gc-success-deep` (`#177a42`), which is
  already in the palette for exactly this reason and gives 5.39:1 against white and
  5.03:1 for the white glyph on it. The Wash-First Rule already assigns the deep
  variants to dots and glyphs; this badge is the one place that kept the bright fill.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] Entering Success announces "Gate Connect", not "You're connected"

- **Location:** `src/screens/Success.tsx:41-45` and `src/components/gc/PopHeader.tsx:28-31`; consumed at `src/App.tsx:544`
- **Category:** Accessibility
- **Evidence:** `document.querySelector("[data-screen-focus]")` takes the first
  match in DOM order. Success renders `PopHeader` (whose `<h1>` carries the
  attribute) before its own `<h1>`, so focus lands on the wordmark. Success is the
  only screen with the collision: `OrgPicker` puts its two candidates in mutually
  exclusive branches, and Settings/GroupDetail use `SubHeader` alone.
- **Impact:** The screen-change announcement the focus move exists to produce says
  "Gate Connect" on the one screen that has news to deliver. Also two `<h1>`
  elements on one screen.
- **WCAG:** 2.4.3 Focus Order (A), and 1.3.1 for the duplicate h1
- **Recommendation:** Scope the query to the last match, or drop
  `data-screen-focus` from PopHeader on screens that carry their own heading. The
  cleanest version is for `PopHeader` to accept the attribute as a prop that Success
  declines, since Home genuinely wants it.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] The 22px switch overlaps a full-row target

- **Location:** `src/components/gc/ui.tsx:101-119`; rows at `src/screens/Home.tsx:485-545` and `src/screens/GroupDetail.tsx:261-301`
- **Category:** Accessibility
- **Evidence:** The switch is `h-[22px] w-[38px]`, 2px under the 24px minimum. It
  paints over a sibling `absolute inset-0` button that covers the entire row. A 24px
  circle centered on the switch necessarily intersects that target, so the Spacing
  exception cannot rescue it, and a switch is not inline text so the Inline exception
  does not either.
- **Impact:** Missing the family switch by 2px vertically opens the detail panel
  instead of routing the family. Both outcomes are plausible intents, so the user
  gets no error, just the wrong screen. This is the app's primary control on its
  primary screen.
- **WCAG:** 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum), AA in WCAG 2.2. Outside the 2.1 target on
  record, but the mis-activation is real regardless of which version is cited.
- **Recommendation:** Keep the locked 38x22 visual geometry and grow only the hit
  area: `before:absolute before:-inset-y-1 before:content-['']` on the switch button
  gives a 30px tall target with no pixel changed. DESIGN.md locks the appearance,
  not the hitbox.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P2] The key field's accessible name is its own placeholder

- **Location:** `src/components/gc/ui.tsx:167-171`; `src/screens/FirstRun.tsx:161-171`, `src/screens/Settings.tsx:449-452`
- **Category:** Accessibility
- **Evidence:** Neither call site passes `aria-label` or associates a `<label>`.
  FirstRun's visible "Gate API Key" (line 161) is a styled `<div>`. Chromium's name
  computation falls back to `placeholder`, so the field announces as "sk-gw-…, edit
  text, protected" on FirstRun and "Enter new sk-gw-… key" in Settings.
- **Impact:** The name is an example value rather than a label, and it disappears
  from the visible field the moment the user types, so a screen-reader user
  re-entering the field has only the placeholder-derived name to go on. Settings'
  string happens to read as an instruction and survives better than FirstRun's.
- **WCAG:** 1.3.1 Info and Relationships (A), 3.3.2 Labels or Instructions (A)
- **Recommendation:** Give `Input` an `id`, make the existing visible text a
  `<label htmlFor>`, and keep the placeholder as the format hint it already is.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] The update panel's version chevron is 2.42:1

- **Location:** `src/components/UpdatePanel.tsx:233`
- **Category:** Accessibility
- **Evidence:** `text-gc-ink-5` (`#a1a6bb`) on white: **2.42:1**. This is the only
  `text-gc-ink-5` in the product; every other glyph uses ink-4 at 3.97:1 or darker.
- **Impact:** The chevron is what turns "v1.4.2 v1.5.0" into a direction. Losing it
  leaves two version numbers with no stated relationship, in the panel that asks the
  user to accept a change.
- **WCAG:** 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (AA)
- **Recommendation:** `text-gc-ink-4`, matching every other chevron in the app.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

### [P2] The reveal-key control is a 14x14 target

- **Location:** `src/components/gc/ui.tsx:176-184`, `src/screens/Settings.tsx:437-444`
- **Category:** Accessibility
- **Evidence:** Both buttons carry no box classes at all, so their hit area is the
  14px icon. Every other icon control in the app goes through `IconButton`, which is
  `h-7 w-7` (28px). These two are the exceptions.
- **Impact:** Half the size of the equivalent control everywhere else, inside a 36px
  input row, for the action that decides whether a secret is on screen. The Spacing
  exception is a marginal pass (the input's box is 8px away and the circle stops 3px
  short), so this is more ergonomics than strict failure.
- **WCAG:** 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum), 2.2 AA
- **Recommendation:** Route both through `IconButton`, which already carries the
  size, hover wash and focus ring. `Input` would need to stop imposing its own
  `gap-0.5` spacing on the trailing slot.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P3] 18 truncation clauses lose content rather than wrap

- **Location:** `src/screens/Home.tsx` (6), `Settings.tsx` (3), `GroupDetail.tsx` (3), `OrgPicker.tsx` (2), `App.tsx` (2), `FirstRun.tsx` (1), `PopHeader.tsx` (1)
- **Category:** Responsive Design
- **Impact:** Each one is individually defensible at 360px, and the ledger's
  exception line was already converted to `line-clamp-2` for exactly this reason.
  Collectively they are the mechanism by which the text-growth failure above becomes
  invisible: the app never reports that it shortened anything.
- **Recommendation:** Audit them in one pass for which carry identifiers the user
  may need to read in full (the gateway host, the org name, the credential-store
  name) and give those wrapping or a title attribute.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P3] Three mechanism chips sit below the documented type ramp

- **Category:** Theming

### [P3] Five `fill="#002a5f"` literals bypass the token

- **Location:** `src/App.tsx:924`, `src/components/gc/PopHeader.tsx:33`, `src/screens/FirstRun.tsx:110`, `src/screens/OrgPicker.tsx:86`, `src/screens/Success.tsx:36`

- **Evidence:** `ConstellationHexMark` already defines `const NAVY = "#002a5f"`

- **Recommendation:** Default the prop to `NAVY` and drop the argument at all five
  sites. Also `src/screens/Onboarding.tsx:78` uses
  `drop-shadow-[0_14px_34px_rgba(20,38,102,0.5)]`, whose color is in no ramp.

### [P3] Two `setTimeout` calls outlive their components

- **Location:** `src/components/gc/ui.tsx:284-286`, `src/screens/GroupDetail.tsx:81-83`
- **Category:** Performance
- **Impact:** Both reset a "Copied" flag after 1600ms with no cleanup. Dismissing
  the error note or collapsing the row inside that window sets state on an unmounted
  component. Harmless in React 18, but it is a real leak in the two places the user
  is most likely to be closing something.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P3] `IconButton` does not require an accessible name

- **Location:** `src/components/gc/ui.tsx:57-75`
- **Category:** Accessibility
- **Evidence:** The props type does not require `aria-label`, and the button's only

  is a latent gap rather than a live defect.
- **Recommendation:** Make `aria-label: string` required in the props type. The next
  call site then cannot forget.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P3] The gateway-URL copy button never announces success

- **Location:** `src/screens/Settings.tsx:368-373`
- **Category:** Accessibility
- **Evidence:** The icon swaps `copy` to `check` on success while `aria-label` stays

- **Recommendation:** Swap the label with the icon, or point a small `role="status"`

- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P3] Two tour images have no intrinsic dimensions

- **Location:** `src/screens/Onboarding.tsx:24-27`, `:99-103`
- **Category:** Performance
- **Impact:** No `width`/`height`, so the tour's first paint reflows when each PNG

- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable optimize`

### [P3] `buildGroups` runs twice per render

- **Location:** `src/App.tsx:913` and `src/screens/Home.tsx:73`
- **Category:** Performance
- **Impact:** App computes the grouped ledger for the group screen, then Home
  recomputes it from the same three inputs. With the product capped at three
  families this is a few hundred operations and invisible; it is listed because it is
  the kind of duplication that stops being free if the cap ever lifts.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable optimize`

## Findings from the prior critique round that this audit does not sustain

Three items carried forward from critique round 13 do not survive verification.
Recording them so they stop being re-raised.

- **`aria-hidden` over focusable content (`App.tsx:1129-1131`) is not a violation.**
  `useFocusTrap` handles Tab in both directions, swallows Tab when the panel has
  nothing focusable, re-places focus on each step change via `resetKey`, and binds
  on `document` in the capture phase so it works even when focus has escaped. The
  four takeovers render as siblings *above* the hidden subtree, not inside it, and
  `pointer-events-none` closes the mouse path. Focus cannot reach the hidden
  content.
- **The `interacted`-gated live region (`Home.tsx:201-209`) is not an SC 4.1.3
  failure.** The case it suppresses is the backend's startup auto-enable, and that
  path already renders a `role="status"` banner ("Routing is on. Anything already
  open isn't routing through Gate yet"), which is announced. The gate removes a
  duplicate announcement, not the only one.
- **`overflow-x-hidden` clipping at 200% zoom (`App.tsx:1137`) is the wrong frame.**
  Zoom is not reachable in this window at all: `resizable: false` and no zoom
  hotkeys. The real finding is the absence of any resize path, filed above as the
  1.4.4 P1, with `overflow-x-hidden` as its aggravating factor rather than its
  cause.

## Patterns & Systemic Issues

1. **Focus vocabulary is centralized for buttons and nowhere else.**
   `index.css:27-30` declares one ring for every `button:focus-visible`, explicitly
   because 32 of 38 controls were hand-written inline buttons falling back to
   Chrome's UA ring. Inputs never got the same treatment, and inputs are where the
   app handles secrets.
2. **Absolute px is total.** 130 font-size literals, 0 `rem`. This is what makes
   the type ramp enforceable by the detector and it is also why there is no text
   scaling story. The two facts are the same decision.
3. **Icon controls are consistent except at exactly two sites.** `IconButton` is
   28px with a hover wash and a focus ring, used 8 times with a label each time. The
   two reveal-key buttons bypass it and are 14px with neither.
4. **Status colors are correctly split into bright and deep variants, with one
   holdout.** `success-deep`, `warning-deep` and `error-deep` all exist for exactly
   the contrast reason, are documented in the Wash-First Rule, and are used
   correctly everywhere the audit checked. The Success badge is the one place still
   on the bright fill.

## Positive Findings

- **Contrast discipline is genuinely above average.** 34 of 45 computed pairs pass,
  and the passes are not accidental: `switch-off` was chosen against two surfaces,
  the three `*-deep` variants exist specifically to carry text on their own washes,
  and `error-deep` is used for words while `error` stays on dots.
- **The focus traps are the best code in the a11y surface.** `initialFocus` pointing
  at the safe choice rather than DOM order, `resetKey` re-placing focus when a step
  swaps its contents, verifying that `.focus()` actually took (disabled elements
  silently no-op), and the panel itself as the last fallback so focus never lands on
  `body`.
- **The ledger rows are readable without eyes.** The count, the pill and the
  exception all live in `pointer-events-none` spans under a stretch button, which
  would normally hide them entirely; `aria-describedby` pointing at an `sr-only` node
  that carries the whole sentence is the right fix and it is tested.
- **Fonts are bundled, not fetched.** Five local woff2 subsets, no runtime request,
  which is both a performance and a privacy property and is commented as such.
- **Every image has descriptive alt text**, including the platform captures, whose
  alt strings describe what the screenshot shows rather than naming the file.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion` is honored** and the two panel animations degrade to
  instant without losing the state change, since the panel content swaps wholesale.
  The global `0.01ms` collapse is a blunt instrument but it costs nothing here: no
  animation in this app carries information that the static end state does not.
