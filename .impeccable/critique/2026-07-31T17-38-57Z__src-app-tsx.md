## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Genuinely specific: named enforceable rules (Seam, Wash-First, One Room), a bespoke shadow vocabulary, the honest tri-state pill grammar, one signature odd color. Two cracks: the motion grammar DESIGN.md calls signature exists only in onboarding/takeovers (main popover navigation hard-swaps screens with no slide, so "panels slide" is an unbuilt promise), and the provisional dual-palette identity shows in small seams (8px icon tiles vs the locked 9px).

**Deterministic scan (CLI):** 3 findings, all accepted/ignored per the ignore file. Zero unaccepted. Clean for the second round running.

**In-page detector (headless, first run ever):** injected detect.js into the live page via the headless harness (no user-visible overlay; the Chrome extension remains unavailable). 5 findings: low-contrast text #7a7f93 on #ffffff at 4.0:1 (corroborates the ink-4 issue below), 10.5px functional text ("Tools" section label, the v0.4.2 footer) under the engine's 11px floor, a cramped-padding div, and flat-type-hierarchy (1.5:1 ratio across the popover ramp). Judgment on those: the 10.5px mono labels are the documented Label step (system decision, keep), the flat hierarchy is the deliberate one-room compact ramp, but the low-contrast hit is real and matches Assessment A's finding.

**Render evidence:** all 11 scenario screenshots render cleanly; only intentional ellipsis truncations; no clipped glyphs or broken layout.

## Overall Impression

The craft floor is now high and mechanically clean; what's left is architectural coherence. The per-tool toggle (added this round by request) is the right direction but created three routing control planes (master, provider, tool) whose relationship no screen explains, and the product's own "one toggle per provider" positioning now disagrees with its newest UI. The remaining pixel-level debt is small and specific: ErrorNote title contrast, two meaningful ink-4 strings, screen-reader structure.

## What's Working

1. **Truth-telling as a design system.** The partial pill refuses to round up to green; the stale-address banner stays sticky because the tools stay broken until acted on. Status honesty is engineered.
2. **Destructive-action choreography.** Non-stacking inline confirms, the quit flow's reversible middle path, forget aborting rather than stranding traffic.
3. **Takeover discipline.** Z-ordered deferral, background aria-hidden, a real focus trap with focus restoration.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Three control planes, no visible relationship.** Master switch (Home), provider switches (Routing), tool switches (ToolDetail) all write routing state; disconnecting a tool then visiting Routing shows a provider switch state with no explanation, and the positioning claim ("one toggle per provider") now disagrees with the newest UI. Fix: list covered tools as a mono sub-line on each provider row; make ToolDetail's Provider row state the coupling. (ProxyScreen.tsx:153-175, ToolDetail.tsx:125-139). Command: /impeccable clarify
2. **[P1] ErrorNote title fails AA and the system's own rule.** gc-error on gc-sunken is ~3.3:1 at 11.5px; DESIGN.md says status color never carries body text. Fix: title in ink, dot/icon carries the red. (ui.tsx:200-202). Command: /impeccable polish
3. **[P1] Screen-reader structure and focus continuity.** No landmarks/headings outside takeovers; setScreen moves focus nowhere; switches disable mid-flight and eject keyboard focus. Fix: real heading elements, focus the panel header on entry, aria-busy instead of disabled during pending. (App.tsx screen switch, ui.tsx). Command: /impeccable harden
4. **[P2] "Close agents…" lands three clicks from closing agents.** The banner action opens the takeover in its informational state (primary: "Got it"). Fix: preset the confirm step when entered via the banner. (App.tsx onCloseAgents, StartupRoutingNotice.tsx). Command: /impeccable distill
5. **[P2] ink-4 carries meaningful text (4.0:1 on white, confirmed by the in-page detector).** QuitConfirm's reassurance sentence and the header workspace host. Fix: promote to ink-3. (QuitConfirm.tsx:78, PopHeader.tsx:24). Command: /impeccable polish

**Sam:** zero headings/landmarks on the main screens; panel changes silently drop focus to body; toggling ejects focus via disabled; ErrorNote title and QuitConfirm's explanation below AA; the raw-details summary is an 11px target.

## Minor Observations

- Onboarding's ink-filled primary button vs the popover's accent primaries: two grammars.
- The z-order comment in App.tsx says z-20 vs z-30 but StartupRoutingNotice is z-10.
- Home's empty state explains the proxy-vs-config split that PRODUCT.md claims users never see; transparency or positioning drift, pick one.
- Unavailable providers render a disabled switch with no reason.
- Two 404s on page load in the dev console (worth a look).

## Questions to Consider

1. Which toggle is the product now: per-provider (the positioning claim) or per-tool (the newest UI)? One of them is legacy wearing the flagship label.
2. Onboarding promises prompt-injection blocking, redaction, and compression; the popover never shows evidence of any of it. Is an unverifiable safety claim building trust or spending it?
3. Why is "restart your agents" the user's chore? The app can close agents, count them, and detect stale routing; what would it take for the gatekeeper to finish the job, or notice when the user has?
