## Design Specificity Verdict

**Specific in vocabulary, and measurably less specific in composition than last
round.**

**LLM assessment.** The language is unmistakably authored for this product:
`desired` vs `routed` (a distinction almost nothing else needs and this cannot
survive without), "set up elsewhere" as a first-class state, `config file` /
`proxy` mechanism chips, per-platform vault names kept distinct for the secret
store and the trust store. None of that is category furniture.

The composition moved the other way this session. Home is now a status card with
a drill-in row: the exact shape a VPN client, a backup agent or a sync tool
ships. The ledger is a stock accordion. Three requested structural changes each
traded a product-specific composition for a conventional one, while the
vocabulary stayed as specific as ever. The tell is the new panel: 174px of
content in a 487px room, reached by a click, holding two rows.

**Deterministic scan.** `detect.mjs --json` over `src`, `index.html`,
`tailwind.config.ts`: **8 findings, all 8 dropped per `ignore.md`, 0
surviving.** Every one re-verified at source, including the two
`oklch(0.165 0 0)` seam literals in `tailwind.config.ts` and the four
`mask-image` `#000` stops. The ignore list is masking no drift. The detector
found nothing the review missed; the review found nothing the detector should
have caught.

**Visual overlays.** None available, and none claimed. No browser tab tool is
exposed, so injection was never attempted.

## Overall Impression

The one-room promise is now genuinely kept: family, member, mechanism and remedy
are all reachable without stacking a screen, and the chevrons rotate at both
levels to say so. That is real craft and it landed this session.

The cost landed too, and it is concentrated in one place. The ledger's own panel
holds 174px of content in a 487px room and charges a navigation for it. Home,
which measurement shows is fine at 26% empty when connected, gave up the three
pills that were its reason to exist and got one line of text back. The biggest
opportunity is not to fill the void with decoration; it is to decide whether that
panel deserves to be a panel at all when the product caps at three families.

## What's Working

1. **The two-level accordion.** One family open at a time, chevrons rotating at
   both levels, the family's error banner appearing in place above the member
   that caused it, and the member's own disclosure carrying mechanism, host,
   plain-language explanation, payload with a Copy button and the remedy. The
   hierarchy reads correctly after the indent and weight fix.
2. **The door reports rather than just links.** Ranking failure over blocked
   certificate over setup-elsewhere means Home still answers "is anything
   wrong?" in error-deep ink after losing the pills. That was the right
   mitigation for a real regression.
3. **Error prevention by construction.** `desired` vs `routed` makes the
   destructive-toggle bug impossible rather than guarded, and the comment records
   the bug that taught it.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The routing-off door drops the inventory and repeats the card**
- **What:** With routing off, every member reports `attention: "master-off"`, so
  the door's ranked exception prints "waiting on routing" and the family names
  never render. Directly above it the card says "Off · 2 waiting".
- **Why it matters:** Routing-off is the state where "what comes back when I flip
  this?" is the only question, and the screen answers it twice in the same words
  while withholding the answer. It is also the state a first-run user lands in.
- **Fix:** Drop `master-off` from the door's ranked exceptions, keeping error,
  needs-trust and drifted. The master card already owns its own state; the door
  should fall through to the family names.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] The ledger panel does not earn a screen**
- **What:** 174px of content in a 487px viewport with two families (64% empty),
  336px with one expanded (31%). It costs a click from Home, and `groups.ts`
  records that the product caps at three families because every agent harness is
  hidden pending validation.
- **Why it matters:** A room built for a crowd that is not coming. The user pays
  a navigation to see two rows, and Home paid three pills for it.
- **Fix:** A composition decision, not a silent revert. Either give the panel
  work worth a screen (the certificate ceremony belongs here more than on Home;
  the per-family detail could open expanded by default at two families), or put
  the ledger back on Home and keep the accordion there. I would not fill it with
  decoration.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape`

**[P2] The chevron means two things one screen apart**
- **What:** Home's door chevron navigates; the ledger's row chevrons expand in
  place. Round 13 flagged exactly this pattern between Home and GroupDetail; the
  fix removed it there and reintroduced it here.
- **Why it matters:** It is the one glyph the user has to learn, and it now
  teaches the wrong lesson on whichever screen they meet second.
- **Fix:** Give the door a different affordance from a row's expander. The
  cleanest is that a navigating row shows a chevron and an expanding row shows a
  disclosure caret that starts pointing down, or the door drops the chevron for
  its own icon since it is a card rather than a list row.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

**[P2] A member name truncates in the state that needs reading**
- **What:** Measured: "Claude Desktop / Cowork" clips at 151.2px in the
  needs-trust state. The 10px indent I added for hierarchy plus the wide "Needs
  trust" pill did it; the name fit before the indent.
- **Why it matters:** The needs-trust member is the one the user came to act on,
  and it is the one whose name gets cut.
- **Fix:** Let the member name wrap to two lines like the family's exception
  line, or shorten the pill's label at member level where the row's own
  disclosure already explains the state.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] The banner is the loudest thing on Home, on nearly every launch**
- **What:** `staleAgentsCount` returns 1 when its probe fails, and routing is
  restored at startup, so the "Routing is on. Anything already open isn't routing
  through Gate yet" notice shows on most launches. On a screen that is now 26%
  empty it is the largest, brightest element, above the control it comments on.
- **Why it matters:** The emotional target is "good, that's handled". The
  peak-end of nearly every session is a yellow warning.
- **Fix:** Make the probe's failure default quiet rather than loud, and let the
  notice sit below the card it comments on rather than above it.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable quieter`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user):** Home → door → family → member is three clicks to reach a
member that took two last round. No bulk "route everything" although
`setGroupRouted` is already a per-member loop. No keyboard shortcut to the master
switch. Opens the ledger panel and finds two rows in a screen-sized surface.

**Sam (screen reader, keyboard):** Two levels of nested disclosure to reach a
remedy. With one family open there are four switches on screen, each overlapping
a row-sized expand target (measured nearest-neighbour gap: 0 for all four). The
family flip announces via a live region; the member's state lives in a
description that is read on focus, not on change.

**Jordan (first-timer):** Opens the popover and sees a card, a hostname and a
link. What the product actually does for them is one line of family names behind
a chevron. The word "routing" appears four times before anything names a tool
they recognise.

**Riley (stress tester):** Confirmed reachable and handled: 74-character staging
host clamps with a title; a 45-character tool name wraps to two lines; the last
tool uninstalling while the ledger is open shows a sentence rather than a blank
panel; four families overflow with a fade cue. Not handled: no undo after "Close
them", and the door's ranked exception hides the second-worst problem entirely.

## Minor Observations

- "Nothing routing" in the pill over "On · 0 of 1 routing" in the card is the
  same fact twice, 40px apart.
- Settings' "Gateway" row label still carries no information; adjudicated
  acceptable in the clarify pass because environment is not reliably derivable.
- OrgPicker marks no current org, so "Switch organization" opens a list with no
  you-are-here.
- The family row and its member rows are the same control at two levels; the
  indent and font-weight fix helps but a member is still a 44px row with a pill,
  a switch and a chevron, exactly like its parent.
- `text-[14.5px]` on the dashboard link is the ramp step nearest the 15px an
  outside edit asked for; if 15px was deliberate the ramp should gain the step
  rather than the file drifting from it.

## Questions to Consider

- If the product caps at three families, what is the panel for? What would it
  hold that makes the click worth paying?
- Home has 126px of room in its busiest state. What belongs there that is not a
  notice?
- The pill was called the most important pixel on the screen. It is now two
  navigations from the tray. Was the pill wrong, or is the door?
- What would this look like if the accordion lived on Home and there were no
  second surface at all?
