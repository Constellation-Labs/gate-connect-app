## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Genuinely specific: a named creative idea, a bespoke elevation vocabulary, named rules enforced in code (Wash-First on every pill, Mono-Earns-Its-Place, once-per-install takeover with storage-failure fallback). The one identity tension (provisional indigo vs the parent ink-primary rule) is self-documented.

**Deterministic scan (CLI):** 3 findings, all accepted/ignored. Zero unaccepted, third clean round.

## Overall Impression

The merge worked: steady-state Home is a clean, honest, one-room ledger and the score is the highest yet (32). What remains clusters in two places: worst-case transitional states (stacked cards/banners can push the ledger below the fold at the exact low-attention moment) and the day-30 emotional misfit, where the healthiest possible state, routing restored and humming, greets the daily user with a caution-tinted banner every session.

## What's Working

1. **Truthful-state grammar consistently applied:** Partly routed, Set up elsewhere, Needs trust, Coming soon; positioning made pixel.
2. **Accessibility engineered, not sprinkled:** focus traps with return, aria-hidden keyed to takeovers, heading focus on panel change, busy switches that keep keyboard focus, reduced-motion as global contract.
3. **Error copy that respects the audience:** plain titles, next steps, raw payload behind a disclosure, full error text in-popover.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Steady-state nag banner.** startupRoutingHint fires whenever routing is on with agents running at webview start, so the daily user sees a caution banner every session; the success metric's target state reads as a warning. **Fix:** only show when routing came up after the agents did (backend signal), or once per routing-state change with persisted dismissal; at minimum demote to the Routing card subtitle. (App.tsx:281, Home.tsx:220-244). Command: /impeccable quieter
2. **[P1] Worst-case stacking buries the ledger.** Up to 2 cards + 2 banners + an error note render above Tools in ~520px. **Fix:** cap pre-ledger chrome (fold the launch-at-login tip into the routing subtitle or Settings; collapse coexisting hints into one strip). (Home.tsx:140-279). Command: /impeccable distill
3. **[P2] CA card gating disagrees with the pill.** Card shows on proxyOn && !caTrusted, but partial additionally requires anyDomainOn, so with no app rows on, the header is green while a warning card demands action. **Fix:** gate the card on anyDomainOn too. (Home.tsx:111-112, 177). Command: /impeccable clarify
4. **[P2] ink-4 still carries real identifiers below AA** (4.0:1, confirmed in-page): Settings gateway URL mono line, OrgPicker slug/role, FirstRun gateway footer. **Fix:** those lines to ink-3. Command: /impeccable polish
5. **[P3] Noun drift** (agents vs tools vs apps) and **[P3] drifted overwrite has no pause** (one flip replaces a hand-written setup, even from the Home row where the explanatory copy isn't visible). Commands: /impeccable clarify, /impeccable harden

## Cognitive Load

Failed 3 of 8: one primary action per screen (CA state: Trust button vs master switch vs banner action), max-4 options (worst-case Home 12+ interactive choices; Settings legacy ~10; FirstRun expanded ~7), consistent nouns. The merge did NOT overload steady-state Home; it overloaded the transitional states.

## Emotional Journey

- Success screen peak and QuitConfirm end-peak both land well; CA trust remains the best-handled scary moment.
- Day-30 is the valley: the healthiest state greeted with a caution banner every launch.
- The once-per-install takeover teaches whichever direction happens first; the other direction's lesson never comes (minor).

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex:** one global proxyBusy serializes everything with a dead-UI wait cursor; no bulk "route everything"; three flips = three waits.

**Sam:** the reopen update banner has no live region (can appear silently); onboarding step changes announce nothing (dots aria-hidden, no heading focus); ink-4 identifiers at 4.0:1.

**Mid-task developer:** worst case scrolls past a cert card and two banners to reach the pill that answers "why isn't my tool connecting"; "switch on + Not routed pill" on app rows when the master is off requires deducing the dependency; a hurried tap aimed at a row switch can open the detail instead.

## Minor Observations

- "On · 3 routing" has no denominator; "3 of 8 routing" scans better.
- ToolDetail's upstream URL lacks the copy affordance Settings' gateway URL has.
- StartupRoutingNotice's name no longer describes it (toggle takeover).
- "Coming soon" is the one roadmap label in a truth-only pill grammar.
- Onboarding's safety claims (injection blocking, redaction) still unevidenced anywhere in the product.
- The version footer inside the scroll body acts as a phantom "more content" cue.

## Questions to Consider

1. If sustained daily routing is the metric, why does the healthiest morning open with a caution banner instead of a quiet green ledger?
2. The Gatehouse story says the key hangs visibly on its hook, yet Home never mentions the keychain. Should the ledger carry one line of the credential story?
3. When the master is off, eight row switches remain visible and half-meaningful. Dead switches, or a master-only room until the gate opens?
