## Overall Impression

The composition problem round 15 opened with is fixed, honestly and to the
decimal. Home carries its four pills, the exception is ranked first, the room
holds it with 46.2px to spare, Linux fits, and the healthy state finally produces
the "good, that's handled" beat PRODUCT.md asks for. Credit that fully; the
vertical-budget argument that justified the door has been paid off rather than
argued away.

What replaces it is subtler and, in one case, worse: **the fixes landed one level
too high.** The ring ladder went onto the family pill and not onto the member
pill, which is the only place in the app where an intent control and a reality
report sit side by side and can contradict each other, and which is the level
round 15 actually measured. `pointer-events-none` went onto Home's row wrapper
and not onto the ledger's, so 65% of every member row is dead to the mouse.
`groupPillLabel` learned the `error` branch and not the `needs-trust` or
`master-off` branches, so grey now means three different things on the element
the product calls its most important pixel. Each is one level below where the
attention went.

**The single biggest opportunity:** finish the round at the member level. The ring
ladder, the pointer-events fix, and two branches in `groupPillLabel` are all
copies of decisions this codebase has already made and written down one level up.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] 65% of every ledger member row is dead to the mouse

**What.** `GroupMembers.tsx:283` puts an `absolute inset-0` stretch button behind
each member row, box **360 x 74.8**. Measured hit area by 1px grid sweep:
**360 x 26**. A row scan at x=180 down the "Claude Code details" row:

```
y=121..169  MISS  -> DIV.relative.flex.items-center.gap-2.5.py-2.5.pl-6.pr-3.5
y=172..193  HIT   -> BUTTON.absolute.inset-0.cursor-pointer...
```

The upper **49px**, the line carrying the tool name, the pill and the switch, is
intercepted by `GroupMembers.tsx:285`, a `relative` flex wrapper that is **not**
`pointer-events-none`. Its three children are individually (lines 286, 296, 299),
but the wrapper's own `py-2.5` padding band is a live hit target. I verified this
in source independently of the measurement: `Home.tsx:689` reads
`"pointer-events-none relative flex items-center gap-2.5 px-3.5 py-2.5"` and
`GroupMembers.tsx:285` reads `"relative flex items-center gap-2.5 py-2.5 pl-6
pr-3.5"`. One word.

This is the **inverse** of the bug the comment at `GroupMembers.tsx:268-272`
claims to have fixed: "The stretch button used to cover only the upper line, so
the bottom 40% ... did nothing when clicked."

**Why it matters.** The user clicks the tool's name, or its status pill, and
nothing happens, on a row that highlights as one block on hover so it advertises
itself as clickable across its full height. Only the bottom third responds. This
is on the panel that owns every control in the app, and the affected target is
the one the user aims at most naturally. It is not a WCAG 2.5.8 failure (the live
band is still 26px), which is exactly why no contrast or target audit would have
caught it; it took a 2D hit-test sweep.

**Fix.** Add `pointer-events-none` to the wrapper at `GroupMembers.tsx:285` and
keep the `relative` on the switch span at line 299, matching `Home.tsx:689`
exactly. Then add a regression test that hit-tests the row's top edge, because
this class of bug has now been introduced in both directions.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Round 15's intent-versus-reality P1 was reported closed and is 2.6% closed at the level it was measured

**What.** `GroupPill` implements the new severity-weighted ring ladder
(`GroupPill.tsx:57-78`). **`MemberPill` (lines 108-126) applies no `ring-*` class
in any of its five states**; `getComputedStyle(pill).boxShadow === 'none'`,
verified in-page. Measured, switch track against its row versus the pill beside
it against the same row:

| row | intent (switch) | reality (pill as object) | gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home, `home-on`, Routed | 5.98:1 | ring **1.54:1** | **3.88x** (was 5.4x) |
| Home, `home-partial`, Partly routed | 5.98:1 | ring 1.91:1 | 3.13x |
| **ledger `home-off`, "Waiting on routing"** | **5.68:1** (`aria-checked=true`) | **1.08:1, no ring** | **5.26x** |
| ledger `home-error`, "Error" beside an on-switch | 5.68:1 | 1.16:1, no ring | 4.90x |

The `home-off` member row is the precise case DESIGN.md's "Intent versus Reality"
section is written about, a switch reading `on` over a channel where nothing
flows. There the gap is **5.26x against round 15's 5.4x: a 2.6% improvement.**

Worse, and neither assessment expected this: **the ladder's top rung is never
rendered anywhere in the product.** `groupPillLabel` returns "Error" only when
`group.routed === 0` *and* a member has `attention === "error"`, which no state in
the catalogue produces. So the 2.95:1 Error chip DESIGN.md's prose is proudest of
is unreachable, and the Error pill a user actually sees in `home-error` is the
**member** pill at **1.16:1 with no ring**. The header pills carry no ring either
(1.10-1.14:1).

**Why it matters.** DESIGN.md's Pill Seam Ladder is written under `### Status
Pills` as a rule for the component, and its own closing bullet names the member
case explicitly: "On the ledger panel a pill sits beside a switch that reports
intent, and the two are allowed to disagree." The level where they are allowed to
disagree is the level with no ring. The product declares the pill its most
important pixel; on the rows where the claim is load-bearing, the eye receives
"on" from a saturated indigo track and the truth sits in the faintest object on
the row.

**Fix.** Copy the ladder into `MemberPill`, mapping its five labels onto the four
existing skins: Error to Error Deep at 65%, Needs trust and Set up elsewhere to
Warning Deep at 45%, Waiting on routing and Not routed to ink-4 at 45%, Routed to
Success Deep at 30%. Give the header pills the same treatment. Separately, decide
whether `groupPillLabel`'s unreachable Error branch is a missing state or dead
code.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

**What.** Measured geometry:

| state | routing card | ledger card | blocker | viewport |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `home-stale` | 79.5 to 168.8 | 202 to **383** | **y 393 to 460.4** | 487 |

Both banners sit **10px below the ledger card's bottom edge**. The certificate

### [P1] Grey "Not routed" reports three different realities on the pill the product calls its most important pixel

**What.** `groupPillLabel` branches on `group.routed` and `attention === "error"`
only. There is no `needs-trust` branch and no `master-off` branch, and on Home the
exception line for both of those kinds is deliberately suppressed
(`Home.tsx:450`). Consequences:

- `home-partial`: OpenRouter is switched **on**, the master is **on**, and the
  certificate is the only thing holding it. Home renders grey "Not routed" with
  no sentence, byte-identical to a family the user switched off. Visible in
  `C16/B-overlay-after.png`: Claude and OpenAI read amber "Partly routed" while
  OpenRouter reads grey "Not routed" in the same certificate-blocked state.
- `home-off`: all four families render grey "Not routed" while 8 of 8 members are
  `desired`. The only place the difference exists is the card's "Off · 8 waiting".

`memberPillLabel` already owns the right word for the second case ("Waiting on
routing"), and DESIGN.md already owns the state for the first: "**Partial**: the
honest third state for a system that is genuinely half-on (routing up,
certificate untrusted)". DESIGN.md's own Idle entry states the governing
principle, "A family dark *because something failed* must not borrow the grey it
uses for a switch the user set." The fix landed for `error` and stopped.

**Why it matters.** This is the product's signature risk, on its declared most
important element, in its two most common non-healthy states: a fresh install has
an untrusted CA, and "off for a minute" is the daily off state. The mid-task
user's question is "which of my things is carrying traffic, and if not, why". The
pill answers the first half and, for two of four causes, refuses the second half
while looking confident. It misleads in the reassuring direction: tap Trust and a
row the user read as "you turned this off" turns green.

**Fix.** Two branches in `groupPillLabel`, using vocabulary the app already owns.
When `routed === 0` and any member is `needs-trust`, return "Needs trust" in the
Partial skin. When `routed === 0` and any member is `master-off`, return "Waiting
on routing" in the sunken skin with ink-2 text. Both match what `MemberPill`
already does for the same condition. Neither adds a vocabulary; both delete an
ambiguity, and the row's exception line can stay suppressed because the pill
would carry the fact.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] A hard backend failure reaches PostHog and no human

**What.** `forwardBackendErrors` (`App.tsx:99-105`) drains the Rust-side buffer
and calls `trackError` on each message. Nothing else consumes it; the comment is
explicit ("Drain the backend's buffered failures into the analytics seam").
Verified in the browser: with `drain_backend_errors` returning
`{context: "proxy_enable", message: "failed to bind loopback port 8317: address
in use"}`, the rendered popover is **byte-identical** to `home-on` and the string
appears **zero** times in the DOM.

**Why it matters.** The buffer exists precisely because these failures predate the
webview (startup auto-enable runs before the popover mounts), which means the
class of error it carries is exactly the class the user has no other way to
learn about. The proxy engine failing to bind at startup is a total routing
outage the user experiences as "my tools stopped working and Gate Connect looks
fine". In an otherwise scrupulously honest app, this is the one place it knows
something material and tells only its telemetry, which inverts PRODUCT.md's
principle 1.

**Fix.** Keep the analytics call and add a screen. `classifyError`, `ErrorNote`,
`RawDetail` with Copy, and `backendErrorContext` all already exist, and Home
already renders `ErrorNote` from `providerError`. Set `providerError` from the
first drained error whose context implies routing is down, and place it where a
blocker belongs, above the ledger per the previous issue.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

## Emotional Journey

**Peak: `home-on`, and it is new.** Four green pills, one calm card, "Session in
your keychain", a version number, nothing shouting. This is the beat PRODUCT.md
asks for and round 15 could not produce. The single biggest emotional improvement
this round is that the healthy state now looks handled instead of looking empty.

**End: still leaky.** The last and largest interactive object before the
credential strip is `Gate dashboard`, 143x34 at 14.5px in saturated indigo,
pointing out of the app. It is larger than the `Routing` heading (13.5px) and the
family names (13px). Under peak-end, the daily visit ends on the exit.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (impatient power user).** Home is read-only: unrouting Claude for an hour
costs tap the row, wait 260ms, flip the switch, press Escape, from a screen with
46.2px of vertical slack and no per-row switch. No bulk anything: 8 members means
4 family flips or 8 member flips, and the ledger's 7 switches have no select-all.
The only one-click action on Home leaves the product. `proxyBusy` puts
`cursor-wait [&_*]:!cursor-wait` on the root so nothing can be queued during a
slow enable. Escape is bound only to the two panels; there is no keyboard route to
toggle routing and no keyboard dismissal of the popover. And the `Command-line
tools` switch, the one control that rewrites his shell environment, is below the
fold whenever a family is expanded (150px of overflow in `home-off`).

**Sam (screen reader, keyboard-only, 4.5:1, 200% zoom).** Genuinely strong first,
because it is: all 7 Home controls Tab-reachable, every stop painting a 2px indigo
ring, row buttons carrying `aria-label` plus an `aria-describedby` `sr-only` span
reading "Routed. 4 of 4 routing", pills `aria-hidden` so nothing announces twice,
and level-correct heading outlines on both screens. Failures: **no text can be
enlarged anywhere** (137 hard-coded `text-[Npx]` classes, no `rem` outside one
border radius, `"resizable": false` in `tauri.conf.json`, and no zoom command,
hotkey or `set_zoom` call in `src-tauri/`), which is an unmet WCAG 1.4.4 AA
requirement against PRODUCT.md's explicit AA commitment. Six ledger family
switches report `aria-checked="true"` with no `aria-describedby` while the pill
beside them reads "Not routed". Member-level state changes reach no live region at
all. `Trust` is tabbed after the `What's this?` link 33.2px below it. And the
errored member's switch reads `aria-checked="false"`, asserting an intent the
banner above says the app cannot read.

**Dev (the daily glancer, from PRODUCT.md's own success metric).** Two seconds,
twice a day, to confirm nothing drifted. This is the persona the release is
defined by and the one this build serves worst in exactly the states it was
designed for. `home-stale` and `home-tip` both read green above the fold. Drift,
the failure PRODUCT.md names by name, renders at 11px in ink-2 ("Claude Code set
up elsewhere") with no severity colour, because only `error` gets Error Deep, so
the signal the product exists to catch is the second-quietest thing on the screen.
A total engine failure renders nothing at all. There is no "as of" and no re-check
gesture; the error copy's own instruction is "reopen this window from the menu bar
to re-check", so the refresh gesture is close-and-reopen. And grey "Not routed"
cannot distinguish "you turned this off" from "the certificate is holding it",
which is the entire content of a daily glance.

## Corrections Made During Synthesis

Two claims from the isolated design review did not survive measurement, and the
corrected versions are what appear above:

1. **The blockers are not below the fold.** Assessment A argued that `home-stale`
   and `home-tip` bury their banner "inside the region the fade dims". Measured,
   both banners are **fully visible without scrolling** (bottoms at 460.4 and
   456.4 in a 487px viewport); what actually falls below the fold in those states
   is the `Gate dashboard` link. The ordering finding survives intact and is
   priority issue 3, because the banner still sits below four contradicting green
   pills. The "buried by the fade" aggravation does not.
2. **`home-empty` has 150.8px of slack, not ~300px.** The design review estimated
   roughly 300px of unused space; the measured figure is 150.8px (31.0%). Still
   the largest empty area on a non-transient screen, but half the claimed size.

One measurement error inside the evidence lane is also worth recording, because it
produced a finding: the first hit-area sweep used single-axis line scans, which
reported `hit 0x26` on member rows. The 2D grid re-run is what distinguished the
artifact from the real occlusion, and round 15's probe had read pseudo-element
inset properties that do not exist as CSS property names, so it missed the
switch's `before:-inset-y-1` expansion entirely. All hit numbers above come from
the 2D sweep.
