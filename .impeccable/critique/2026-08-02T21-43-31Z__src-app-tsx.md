## Design Specificity Verdict

The pixels are the stock macOS-menubar-utility dialect: centered hex mark, 17px title, full-width indigo button, shadow-seam cards with a 36px leading tile, 38x22 pill switch. ## Overall Impression

The vocabulary is now excellent and the structure is honest. The state model underneath it is not, and the single biggest opportunity is that the app's most-used control tells a lie the code already knows about.

Two of today's changes (family grouping, one directional notice) did real work: they cut Home from seven rows to four and removed a whole class of contradictory banners. But grouping also made a pre-existing backend gap visible for the first time: because rows now aggregate, "Off · not routing" sits directly above a family row reading "1 of 2 routing" with its switch on. The lie was always there; the new ledger reports it.

The score dropped from 32 to 24. Most of that is not regression, it is a rework that changed what is under review plus findings four earlier rounds missed. One part is self-inflicted: merging Routing into Home and renaming the families made both onboarding hero screenshots obsolete.

## What's Working

**`groupSummary`'s exception contract (`src/lib/groups.ts`).** A summary row that names its exception instead of averaging it away is what almost every dashboard gets wrong. Paired with `GroupPill` never promoting partial to "Routed", it is what earns a four-row Home the right to be the whole screen.

**`GroupDetail`'s inline `explain()` plus raw payload.** Each member answers "what is true now" and "what happens if I flip this" in one sentence in the user's own nouns, with the verbatim `status.reason` in mono underneath. Deleting the third level removed a navigation step without losing a word of reassurance.

**One confirm grammar, no stacked dialogs.** `ConfirmPanel`, `confirmingAdopt`, `confirmReveal`, `confirmingServer` and the takeovers share one inline shape, and each names precisely what is about to be lost. Every focusable across all five views has an accessible name (13/13 on Home, 14/14 partial, 6/6 group detail, 8/8 Settings, 3/3 first run) and every toggle is `role="switch"` with correct `aria-checked`.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The master Routing switch reports a state it does not deliver**
- **What:** `proxy_disable` (`src-tauri/src/lib.rs:681`) calls `provider::snapshot_and_disable_all()`. The doc comment on the sibling `snapshot_and_disable_all_for_quit()` states that the quit sweep exists because the provider pass misses "standalone tools no provider maps (OpenCode and friends)". `App.tsx:610` already documents the same hazard for `setGroupRouted`. Home then renders "Off · not routing" and a header pill "Routing off". Reproduced on screen: that sub-line sitting above a Claude row reading "1 of 2 routing · Partly routed" with its switch on.
- **Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md positions on "one honest switch each" and "status pills are system state, not marketing copy". The most-used control is the one that lies, and the failure mode strands harnesses on a dead relay, which is exactly the "tools drifting off the gateway" churn signal named as the failure condition.
- **Fix:** Point `proxy_disable` at the quit-time sweep so master-off disconnects every managed tool, and have `proxy_enable` restore the same set. Until that lands, compute the sub-line from the ledger instead of `proxy.running`: render "Off · N still routing" whenever `groups.some(g => g.routed > 0)`.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] In the certificate state the ledger is entirely below the fold**
- **What:** `home-partial` measures **816px** of content against a 560px viewport and a 520px budget; `scrollTop` maxes at 256. Above the fold: header, master card, a 60-word trust card with a full-width accent button, and the change banner. Zero complete ledger rows visible. `Home.tsx:70-73` says transient chrome "must never bury the ledger".
- **Why it matters:** "Debugging why a tool isn't connecting" is one of three moments PRODUCT.md names for opening the popover, and an untrusted CA is the most common cause. The answer requires discovering that the body scrolls, in a window with hidden scrollbars.
- **Fix:** Reduce the trust card to a one-line sunken notice and let the row-level "Needs trust" pill carry the detail; move the 60-word explanation into the confirm step behind Trust. Extend the existing one-banner-at-a-time rule to include the trust card.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] The certificate removal promise is false at the moment of consent**
- **What:** `Home.tsx` promises "You can remove it anytime in Settings under Certificate." `Settings.tsx:575` gates that section on `caTrusted && !routingOn`. The user reads the promise while routing is on, taps Trust, and lands at `caTrusted && routingOn`, where the section does not render. Confirmed: no Certificate section anywhere in Settings in that state.
- **Why it matters:** This is a root CA on the user's machine. Breaking the removal promise at the instant they act on it is the worst available failure for a reassuring gatekeeper.
- **Fix:** Drop the `!routingOn` gate. Render whenever `caTrusted`, with Remove explaining that removing it while routing is on stops proxy-routed apps from routing.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The ledger is opaque to screen readers, and two text colours fail AA**
- **What:** Each row's only accessible name is `aria-label={group.name + " details"}`. The count, pill text, and exception live in sibling `pointer-events-none` spans with no `aria-describedby`, so tabbing yields "Claude details, button" and "OpenClaw failed" is never announced. No list semantics. Measured contrast failures: `v0.4.2` at **3.97:1** on all five views and Settings' "Reset" at **3.82:1**; `Input`'s placeholder carries real instruction at ink-4. Group detail, Settings and first run have **zero** `role="status"` or `aria-live` regions, so `RoutingChangeNotice`'s outcome swap is never announced. First run has **no headings at all**.
- **Why it matters:** WCAG 2.1 AA and full keyboard operability are stated first-release targets, and DESIGN.md names ink-3 as the text floor.
- **Fix:** Give each row a description node with an id, point the stretch button at it via `aria-describedby`, wrap the ledger in `role="list"`. Move placeholders and the version footer to ink-3, darken the error red. Add an `aria-live` region to the takeover outcome and an `h1` to first run.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

**[P2] Both onboarding hero screenshots teach a deleted UI**
- **What:** `src/assets/app-integrations.png` shows a "Routing" sub-screen with a back chevron, a `PROVIDERS` section label, and rows "Claude Code / Cowork", "OpenAI / Codex", "OpenRouter", captioned "The Routing screen in Gate Connect". Verified by reading the asset. `where-is-gate-connect-macos.png` shows "Proxy · On · intercepting 3 domains" and a "Direct Gateway" card. Neither surface exists: Routing merged into Home, the label is now "WHAT ROUTES THROUGH GATE", and the families are Claude / OpenAI / OpenRouter / Agent harnesses with status pills.
- **Why it matters:** It is the first thing every new user sees, in a dedicated window, before they open the popover. Teaching a deleted UI makes a trust product feel abandoned.
- **Fix:** Reshoot both from the current build. Align vocabulary too: step 2 says "Protection is a per-app choice" (the app says routing) and "opencode" (the app says "OpenCode").
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

## Cognitive Load

**6 of 8 items fail.** Passing: grouping (the family IA is the strongest move in the app) and progressive disclosure (family, member, inline expansion).

Failing: single focus (master card + trust card + banner + ledger + dashboard link); chunking (Settings has 6 labeled sections plus an unlabeled "Dev mode" orphaned between STARTUP and HELP); visual hierarchy (on `home-partial` the loudest element is "Trust certificate" while the pills are off-screen; master title and row titles are both 13.5px, differing only by weight); one thing at a time; minimal choices; working memory (in the partial state the same fact is stated four times in four wordings: "Partly routed", "On · certificate not trusted yet", the trust card heading, and the row exception).

Decision points over 4 options: **Home with a banner up, 13 interactive targets**; **Settings, 8**, three of them account-destructive; **GroupDetail with 2 members, 6**.

## Questions to Consider

2. DESIGN.md:329 says the pill is a door; the build wraps every pill in `pointer-events-none`. Which one is the design, and how many other sentences in DESIGN.md describe a build that no longer exists?
3. PRODUCT.md's success metric is sustained daily routing, but every screen is composed for setup. What does Home look like on day 40, when the user opens the popover only to confirm it still works? Right now: identical to day 1 minus one banner.
4. "Agent harnesses" is the group the code creates for tools the catalog failed to claim. Is that a family the user recognizes, or the leftovers bin wearing a name?
