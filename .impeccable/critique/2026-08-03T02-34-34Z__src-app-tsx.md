## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Unmistakably this product, and deeper than round 6. The pill vocabulary ("Set up elsewhere", "Needs trust", "Waiting on routing") is a state machine no neighbouring product has, because no neighbouring product routes by both config rewriting and MITM proxy on one ledger. The `desired` vs `routed` split is a product-specific idea: intent and traffic are different facts here in a way they are not in an ordinary settings screen. ## Overall Impression

The state model is now right and the vocabulary is excellent. What is left is that two of round 6's fixes were applied to one branch and not its twin, and the app still has no way to say "you turned something on and nothing is happening."

Two of the four issues below are mine, and specifically half-finished rather than missed: I mapped the cancel verb across five error contexts and skipped `proxy_toggle` and `provider_toggle`, which are the two a user actually hits; and I introduced "Waiting on routing" as a new attention state in the same commit where I gave needs-trust its own remedy button, then left the new state with prose only.

## What's Working

**`desired` vs `routed` as a first-class distinction.** Verified round-tripping cleanly: B's probe reads `true → false → true` on the needs-trust switch with the pill tracking flow separately. Most products would have shipped one boolean and the bug.

**The pinned credential footer.** Platform-named, on every screen, outside the scroll container so it costs nothing, and honest about mode ("Session" vs "Key"). It turns the product's central promise from an onboarding slide into ambient furniture.

**Remedy where the problem is named.** The Trust button inside the expanded needs-trust member is right, and the pattern is good enough that its absence for master-off reads as an oversight.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] A family toggle can announce "Routing is on" while routing is off**
- **What:** `App.tsx:635` (`setGroupRouted`) and `:585` (`setToolRouted`) derive the notice from the toggle's direction, not the resulting state. The two branches have opposite engine effects: `proxy_set_domain` (`lib.rs:795`) only calls `set_domain` and never starts the engine, while `connect_tool` (`lib.rs:131`) calls `manager().enable()`. So flipping a proxy-only family on with the master off enables a domain, routes nothing, and raises "Routing is on. Anything already open isn't routing through Gate yet." directly above "Off · not routing". B's probe, verbatim after the click: header `Routing off`, master `Off · not routing`, row `0 of 1 routing · waiting on routing`, banner `Routing is on.` The same click arms the takeover with `dir: "on"`. `toggleProxy` at `:514` already does this correctly with `next.running`; the master path was fixed and the member paths were not.
- **Why it matters:** The product positions on "one honest switch each", and this is the screen contradicting itself about whether the user's API traffic is being intercepted. The mirror case is quieter and also unflagged: a config-tool toggle silently changes the machine's system-wide proxy state.
- **Fix:** Derive the notice from `proxyStatus()` in the `finally` block, not from intent. Skip it entirely when the proxy was and remains off. When a member toggle does flip the master on, say so; when it does not, say what the ledger already knows: "OpenRouter apps are set to route. Turn routing on to start."
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] "Waiting on routing" names a remedy and ships no control**
- **What:** `explain()` returns "…Turn routing on from Home." and `groupSummary()` puts "waiting on routing" on the row, but B's probe of the expanded member reports **zero buttons** in the expanded region. `needs-trust`, introduced in the same commit, got a Trust button eight lines away.
- **Why it matters:** It is the state a user is most likely to reach by accident, because the family switches are the obvious controls and the master is a separate card. It is now the only attention state with no in-place remedy.
- **Fix:** A "Turn on routing" accent button on any expanded `master-off` member and on the group header when `group.desired > 0 && !proxyOn`, wired to `toggleProxy`. Suppress the close-the-app hint whenever the resulting state has `routed === false`, since closing and reopening changes nothing while the engine is down.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The cancelled-prompt hint still names a button that does not exist, on the two most-used paths**
- **What:** B ran the classifier directly. For `proxy_toggle` and `provider_toggle` the hint is "Click **Connect** again and approve your system password prompt." Those contexts fire from `role="switch"` controls labelled "Route through Gate"; there is no Connect button on Home. `trust_ca` and `forget` are correct.
- **Why it matters:** The enable path prompts for admin every time the system proxy changes, so this is the error copy most users actually read. This is verbatim the bug last round's fix was written to kill, left in place on the paths that matter most.
- **Fix:** Add both contexts to the verb ladder and reword away from "Click" for a switch. Better: put the retry in the `ErrorNote` rather than describing it.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] The new tour hero advertises the staging gateway**
- **What:** `app-integrations.png` is a real capture of the current build, of a build pointed at `gateway-staging.constellationg…`, legible in the mono workspace line. Its bottom edge is a hard square crop mid-card, landing immediately above the pinned footer, so the image illustrating "Your Gate key stays in the operating system keychain" omits the line that says exactly that.
- **Why it matters:** Second thing every new user sees, and it tells them the product ships against staging.
- **Fix:** Recapture against `gateway.constellationgate.ai`, include the footer row, and mask the bottom edge with the same gradient `WHERE_IMAGE` already uses instead of cutting it square.
- **Suggested command:** user-supplied asset

**[P2] Identifiers truncate into nonsense, and the exception truncates out of view**
- **What:** Config members print `member.tool?.default_upstream_url` (full URL with scheme) into the ~90px slot where proxy members print a bare host, yielding `https://api.ant…`. DESIGN.md's Mono rule says mono carries identity and precision; `https:/…` has neither. Separately, `groupSummary` concatenates the exception after the count into one truncating line, so the actionable half is what gets cut: "0 of 2 routing · Codex set up els…". The `sr-only` mirror carries the full sentence, so a screen-reader user is better informed than a sighted one.
- **Fix:** Run config members through the same `hostOf()` the header and Settings use. Drop the redundant count when an exception exists, or give the sub-line a second line.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] A bulk family failure names no tool and hides the culprit**
- **What:** `setGroupRouted` loops members in one `try`; the first rejection aborts the loop and surfaces "Couldn't connect this tool" as a screen-level `ErrorNote` under the master card, naming no member, reporting no partial success, and pushing the failing row off-screen.
- **Fix:** Carry the member name into the error, continue past a failure and report the set at the end, and make the master count honest about unreachable members.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user):** flips the OpenAI switch, sees "0 of 2 routing · Codex set up els…" and a switch that appears to have done nothing, because the family switch deliberately skips the drifted Codex. No bulk affordance; the one control that looks like "everything" is not. At the real 520px height "Open Gate dashboard" is always below a fold with no persistent macOS scrollbar.

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard):** materially better. Real `h1`s with `data-screen-focus`, `h2` section labels, `aria-describedby` carrying the full row sentence, focus traps on all three takeovers, zero unnamed focusables across five views. Remaining: the row switch announces "on" while the pill beside it says "Not routed", and nothing ties flow to the control; no live region announces a routing state change, so flipping the master rewrites the header pill, the card sub-line and four row descriptions in silence; the master card's title is a styled `div`, so the most important control on Home is unheaded.

**Solo developer with no organization** (PRODUCT.md: "No provisioned org can be assumed"): signs in via the primary OAuth path, reaches "No organizations are available for your account. Ask an admin to add you to one." There is no admin, and the only control returns them to the sign-in they just completed. Their two real options, creating an org in the dashboard or falling back to the API-key path, are both invisible. Clearest place where shipped UI contradicts a stated product constraint.

## Minor Observations

- The zero-tools state says "On · nothing enabled yet" over "Nothing to route yet", with the change banner still offering to close apps that cannot be routing.
- "Trust it…" reveals a button called "Trust certificate"; a keyboard user pressing Enter on the first reasonably expects the system prompt.
- Settings' certificate "Remove" is a bare text link with no confirm, while Reset gets a full ConfirmPanel, though Remove's own copy admits it stops apps routing.
- Three confirm grammars coexist; the adopt confirm, which replaces a user's hand-written config, wears the lightest of them.
- The launch-at-login tip lives inside the master Routing card, so that card now holds two unrelated controls.
- OpenClaw's error shows an exact file path and line number and offers no action.
- Only the untrusted-CA state truncates the workspace host, because the amber pill is wider than "Routing on".
- Status-pill dots measure 2.00-2.19:1 against their fills; redundant with the adjacent label, so not raised, but a darker dot would cost nothing.
- `h1` is 16px on Home and 14px on both sub-screens.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the master switch cannot turn everything on, and a family switch can turn the master on, is "Routing" a control or a readout? What breaks if the card becomes a status summary and off-everything moves to where destructive actions already live?
2. Seven rounds in, the untrusted-CA fact is stated four times on one 360px screen. Is that thoroughness, or four rounds each adding a sentence and none deleting one?
3. The tour promises injection blocking, redaction and compression; the popover shows a switch and a keychain line. Is the tour building trust or borrowing it?
4. `GroupDetail` for a one-member family is 90% white. Would the app be better with no second screen at all, given the ledger is three or four rows by design?
5. The footer line is the app's best sentence. Why is it 10.5px grey at the bottom rather than what the header says?
